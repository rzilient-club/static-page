_the pulse of your fleet

Pulzar is the lightweight telemetry agent that runs on every device you manage with _rzilient. It quietly measures what matters — battery, CPU, disk, memory — and turns it into health scores, trends, and alerts on your dashboard.

What it measures

_vital_signs

Every device reports a full picture of its health, identified by its hardware serial number — no manual inventory matching.

batteries.health

Battery health

Charge level, wear, and power consumption — spot batteries that need replacing before employees complain.

cpu.load

CPU load

1, 5, and 15-minute load averages reveal machines that are constantly maxed out or unusually idle.

disk.used_percent

Disk usage

Free space on every drive, so full disks never surprise you during an OS update rollout.

ram.used_percent

Memory pressure

RAM and swap usage identify devices that are under-provisioned for the work they actually do.

temp

Temperature

Thermal readings flag overheating hardware and failing fans early.

system.uptime

Uptime & activity

Uptime, running processes, connected displays, and network activity show how each device is really used.

How it works

_from_device_to_dashboard

Built on the battle-tested Telegraf collector, extended by _rzilient for laptop and desktop fleets.

💻

Collect

A tiny agent samples device vitals every few minutes. It self-registers with the platform and identifies itself by hardware serial.

→
📡

Ship

Metrics are batched and sent securely to the _rzilient platform. Offline? Up to 10,000 readings are buffered locally and delivered when the device reconnects.

→
📊

Understand

The platform turns raw readings into health checks, sparkline trends, anomaly alerts, and inputs to your fleet's security score.

Designed for real fleets

_built_to_be_invisible

Pulzar is engineered to never get in the way of the person using the device.

~10 min Sampling interval with randomized jitter — no synchronized load spikes across the fleet
10,000 Metrics buffered locally when offline, so flaky Wi-Fi never means lost data
0 Keystrokes, screenshots, or file contents captured — Pulzar reads hardware vitals, nothing personal
1 Small native service per device — no browser extensions, no always-on daemons fighting for resources
