Why a Privacy Policy?

Rzilient Group places great importance on the confidentiality and security of your personal data.

The purpose of this Privacy Policy is to inform you in a clear, simple and transparent way about the processing carried out on the personal data you provide to us, or that we may collect when you browse our web or mobile platform www.rzilient.club (the “Platform”), any possible transfer to third parties, and the rights and options you have to control your personal information and protect your privacy.

This policy also explains how we collect, use and protect the data of Rzilient customers (you), visitors, and people who interact with Rzilient via the Platform, emails, or social networks.

What is personal data?

Personal data means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, directly (name, email, address) or indirectly (online identifier, account number, IP address, browsing data, etc.).

Definitions

  • Subscription: contractual model allowing the Client to access maintenance services.
  • Administrator: Client representative authorized to manage an Administrator Account and create/supervise Collaborator Accounts.
  • Client: legal entity acting as a professional customer of Rzilient.
  • Administrator Account: account enabling creation of Collaborator Accounts and access to the Platform services.
  • Collaborator Account: account created by invitation from an Administrator Account holder.
  • User Account: account accessible via Identifiers (includes Administrator and Collaborator accounts).
  • Terms: the Platform terms of use.
  • Collaborator: natural person within the Client organization authorized to use the Platform.
  • Identifiers: your email address and personal password.
  • GDPR: Regulation (EU) 2016/679.
  • Platform: www.rzilient.club.
  • Products: IT equipment available for purchase and/or leasing.
  • Services: Platform features.
  • Telemetry tool: software installed on an employee workstation at the Client’s request/subscription.

Why and how is my data collected and processed?

Rzilient collects personal data through different means:

  • (i) Directly: forms, customer support, communications via Platform/email/social networks, etc.
  • (ii) Third-party services: when you authorize/use third-party services to connect to Rzilient.
  • (iii) Public sources: publicly available information.
